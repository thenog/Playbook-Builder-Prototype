# Jobsites

**Goal**: Let field reps quickly browse, search, and filter active job sites from their phone.

**Key decisions**
- Card-based list, not a table — better for thumb scrolling on mobile
- Search + filter in a persistent top bar so reps can drill down fast
- Status chips (Active, Pending, Completed) for quick visual scan

**Status**: in-progress

**Open questions**
- Should tapping a card navigate to a detail view or expand inline?
- What filters matter most to reps — region, status, rep assignment?
